# Message to the Next Steward

## Purpose

What this handover asks the recipient to do.

## Adoption request

State `adopted`, `partially_adopted`, `not_adopted`, or `undecided`, with scope and reasons.

## Required reading or included material

1. File or section.

## Current state and priority

State what is known, what is underway and the next decision.

## Non-negotiable distinctions

- Evidence is not interpretation.
- Measurements are not estimates.
- Source integrity is not factual correctness.
- Add project-specific constraints.

## Confirmed anchors

List bounded sources and exactly what each establishes.

## Unresolved conflicts and evidence gaps

List conflicts, all material sides, missing evidence and consequences.

## Rejected interpretations

Preserve rejected or superseded theories and why they were rejected.

## Safety, privacy and authority boundaries

State prohibited inference, disclosure and action.

For action-capable or multi-agent work, state the authority-envelope ID, actors and runs, persistent/shared state, intended coordination channels, unresolved boundary events and action-receipt location. If unavailable, record the EvidenceGap.

## Required response

1. Adoption and conformance declaration.
2. Executive critique.
3. Confirmed anchors.
4. Unresolved conflicts and gaps.
5. Proposed changes.
6. Discovery questions.
7. Provenance and uncertainty.
8. Steward-review points.
9. Agentic-profile records where applicable.
