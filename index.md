# Aletheia Knowledge Stewardship Protocol

**Remember well. Reason clearly. Prosper together.**

Aletheia is a portable human-and-AI knowledge stewardship protocol. It preserves evidence, meaning, disagreement, decisions and revision history across people, AI systems and tools.

Its central rule is:

> Knowledge belongs to the project, not the Assistant.

## Current review candidate

Aletheia Protocol 0.5 RC2 includes the accepted optional Agentic Systems Profile for authority, consequential actions, cross-run lineage, persistent shared state and unintended coordination channels. The public licence and canonical repository are confirmed; independent review remains invited before a stable 0.5 release.

Canonical repository: [KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol)

## What Aletheia does not claim

Aletheia is not a truth machine, a guarantee of AI alignment, an authority to act or a method for exposing private chain-of-thought. It makes project knowledge and observable provenance easier to inspect, challenge, revise and hand over.
