# Adoption and AI Handover Guide

## Paste-ready instruction

Attach this release or paste `ALETHEIA_ALL_IN_ONE_AI_BOOTSTRAP_v0.5.md`, then tell the receiving AI:

> Review the supplied Aletheia Protocol 0.5 RC2. State whether you adopt it, partially adopt it, decline it, or remain undecided for this task. Do not merely endorse it: identify contradictions, unnecessary complexity, missing safeguards and interoperability problems. If you use it, separate evidence, observations, measurements, estimates, claims, hypotheses and assumptions; retrieve unresolved conflicts with each challenged node; preserve evidence gaps; propose rather than silently apply normative changes; and finish with a provenance trace and human Steward-review points. If the work is action-capable, persistent or multi-agent, also state whether you adopt the Agentic Systems Profile and identify the applicable authority envelope. Do not claim authority, verification, file access, private intent or capabilities you do not possess.

## Expected response from another AI

Ask it to return:

1. Adoption declaration and scope.
2. Conformance level: Core, Full or Profiled.
3. Executive critique.
4. Requirements it can and cannot follow.
5. Confirmed strengths.
6. Contradictions or ambiguous definitions.
7. Proposed changes in proposal format.
8. A small stress-test using a claim, conflicting source and evidence gap.
9. Provenance and limitations.
10. Matters for the human Steward.

For Agentic Systems Profile review, additionally request actor/run lineage, shared-state and coordination channels, authority boundaries, action receipts, boundary events and substitute-path tests.

## What adoption means

Adoption means using the protocol's distinctions and change discipline for a declared scope. It does not mean accepting the project's claims, granting the AI authority, or asserting permanent memory.

An AI that cannot retain files between sessions can still adopt Aletheia for the supplied context. It should say that persistence depends on the human or host system re-supplying the pack.

An AI MUST NOT infer that adoption authorises external action. Protocol adoption and operational authority are separate declarations.

## Sharing

Share the ZIP for complete review. Use the all-in-one file when an AI accepts only one upload or when copying through a prompt box. Remove or redact private project evidence before broad distribution.

## Receiving proposed improvements

Do not merge suggestions merely because an AI is confident. Convert each material suggestion into a proposal, compare it with the principles and protocol, test it in at least one realistic case, and record the Steward's decision.
